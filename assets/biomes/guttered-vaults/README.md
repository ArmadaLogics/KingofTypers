# Guttered Vaults overworld artwork

Implemented only on Chapter VI's overworld by `js/26-guttered-overworld.js`.

- Active terrain is `terrain-cliff-joins.png` from v132.12. The Chapter I-aligned transparency, native tile coordinates, flat ground, and original v132.1 source PNG are preserved. The corrected atlas gives caps and cliff faces a consistent blue-gray bevel, shares the stone palette with both slope orientations, and blends existing ground texture into the upper and lower ramp surfaces.
- Water uses the chapter's flat `#0b0f16` sea color and the existing shoreline foam.
- Raised tops, cliffs and ramps use the native Chapter I assembly rules with the textured Chapter VI atlas.
- `trail.png`: actual 4x4 trail atlas derived from the reviewed generated sheet. N/E/S/W bits are 1/2/4/8. Matching sockets share identical edge strips. No painted map screenshot is used as a terrain or collision map.
- House/tower/castle: the Wickkeeper Cottage, Tidewatch Spire and Floodlock Citadel from the approved Guttered Vaults concept direction. Active art is restored to the original, unmodified PNGs under `structures-v2/`. The texture redesign in `structures-v3/` was rejected and is retained only as revision history. The citadel PNG has two empty cups. `js/30-guttered-structures.js` draws the approved flame strip at the two cup anchors at 12 fps, with a four-frame phase offset. Reduced motion holds both poses. Stage torches are separate.
- `tree.png`: reviewed Chapter I conifer variant. Three trees frame the pools and east bank. Small groups of shoreline plants leave clear gaps around tree crowns, buildings, paths and ramps. Trees, bushes, and planted shoreline props receive short shadows derived from their base silhouettes.
- From v132.8, the Wickkeeper Cottage uses 10% less contrast around luma 50, with its amber cores preserved. Its soft rear-left shadow is projected from the sprite silhouette, clipped to supporting land, and composited after fixture light.
- From v132.9, both Tidewatch Spires use the same 10% contrast treatment with warm fixture cores preserved. From v132.11, the spires use the citadel’s soft projected shadow with a 5 px offset under the footing. The shadow stays on the receiving shelf.
- From v132.10, Floodlock Citadel uses the v3 proposal’s compact rear-left foundation shadow. Its fade is composited after fixture light and clipped to the receiving elevation.
- Cold Moonlight shades the terrain, buildings, archer and Fletcher. Structures now use the terrain and trees’ shared ambient-color and spatial moonlight pass at a controlled reflected-light exposure. Their original texture and alpha are preserved; amber window cores stay warm. Unlit building material has a deeper night exposure. Each actual amber window/lantern and both blue sconces illuminate the adjacent wall and cast a soft, matching ground pool after the moonlight pass. Ground illumination is clipped to the receiving elevation and terrain alpha; it replaces the earlier generic building-center glows. The emission is steady while the existing flame frames animate. Short foundation shadows are composited into the receiving ground before moonlight, then clipped to matching land/elevation. The Sky setting toggles low blue mist along the banks.
- [`structures-v2/art.json`](structures-v2/art.json) records the active building dimensions, placement offsets and sconce animation. The parent [`art.json`](art.json) records terrain and prop provenance, legacy building specifications and the trail layout.

Terrain geometry: the prepared Chapter III swamp atlas; active surface artwork: the restored v132.1 textured atlas. Props and trail reuse the reviewed art pack. Gameplay map layout and collisions remain native data. The house is moved inland and the reviewed castle shelf is flattened. From v132.13, the small shelf northwest of the cottage has a two-cell east-edge ramp at (10,13) and (10,14). The old opening at (8,14) now bakes as a continuous cliff face. In v132.14 the four remaining interior ramps moved to shelf-edge columns 23, 29, 16 and 24, closing their former cliff-face openings. In v132.15 the four north-bank shelves have distinct footprints. The west spire has a wider terrace, the east spire has a deeper terrace, and the two smaller shelves have a narrower front or a recessed corner. In v132.16, the small northeast shelf steps down to a two-tile front. The lower-middle shelf is shallower and the southeast shelf has a stepped shoulder. All existing ramp cells stay in place. Stage nodes, roads, supplier and combat maps retain their authored positions and rules.

Earlier art sources and prompts are in `Proposals/GutteredVaults/Overworld-Implementation-v1/Source/`.
